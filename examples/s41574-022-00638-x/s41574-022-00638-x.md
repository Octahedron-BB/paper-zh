# Clinical application of intermittent fasting for weight loss: progress and future directions

## Abstract（p1）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract#01 -->
**1.** 摘要 | 间歇性禁食饮食在过去几年中变得非常流行，因为它们能够产生具有临床意义的体重减轻。用最简单的话来说，这些饮食可以定义为禁食期与进食期交替进行。研究最多的间歇性禁食形式包括：隔日禁食（在“禁食日”摄入 0–500 kcal，与“进食日”的自由摄入交替进行）；5:2 饮食（每周两天禁食日和五天进食日）以及限时进食（每天只在规定的时间窗口内进食）。尽管近年来禁食日益流行，但只有少数研究考察了这些饮食对人体的健康益处。本综述的目标是总结这些初步发现，并深入探讨间歇性禁食对人体体重和心脏代谢疾病危险因素的影响。本综述还评估了这些方案的安全性，并就如何将间歇性禁食饮食融入日常生活提供了一些实用建议。此外还提出了对未来研究的建议。

> Abstract | Intermittent fasting diets have become very popular in the past few years, as they can produce clinically significant weight loss. These diets can be defined, in the simplest of terms, as periods of fasting alternating with periods of eating. The most studied forms of intermittent fasting include: alternate day fasting (0–500 kcal per ‘fast day’ alternating with ad libitum intake on ‘feast days’); the 5:2 diet (two fast days and five feast days per week) and time-​restricted eating (only eating within a prescribed window of time each day). Despite the recent surge in the popularity of fasting, only a few studies have examined the health benefits of these diets in humans. The goal of this Review is to summarize these preliminary findings and give insights into the effects of intermittent fasting on body weight and risk factors for cardiometabolic diseases in humans. This Review also assesses the safety of these regimens, and offers some practical advice for how to incorporate intermittent fasting diets into everyday life. Recommendations for future research are also presented.

**2.** 每天。限时进食是指将进食窗口限制在每天特定的若干小时内（通常为 4 至 8 小时），当天其余时间则以水或零热量饮料禁食。限时进食是间歇性禁食的一种独特形式，因为它不要求个体在进食窗口内监测其能量摄入或计算热量。

> time every day. TRE involves confining the eating window to a specified number of hours per day (typically 4 to 8 h), and fasting with water or zero-​calorie beverages for the remainder of the day. TRE is a unique form of intermittent fasting in that it does not require individuals to monitor their energy intake or count calories during the eating window.


#### The effects of intermittent fasting on body weight.（p2）

**1.** 三种主要形式的间歇性禁食对临床结局指标的影响见表 1（隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食）、表 2（限时进食）和图 2。研究若符合以下条件，则被纳入表 1 和表 2 的评估：属于随机试验、在成人中开展、试验持续时间达 5 周或更长，且在设计中将对照组（整个研究期间不改变饮食或身体活动）或每日热量限制组作为比较对象。迄今的研究结果显示，在肥胖男性和女性中，隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食在较短的干预期间（8–12 周）可产生相似程度的体重减轻（较基线减轻 4–8%）。较长期的研究发现，隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食（24–52 周）并未带来比较短期研究更大的体重下降，提示这些饮食的减重效果可能在 12 周时达到峰值。限时进食所实现的体重减轻程度似乎不如隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食显著，在 8–12 周内较基线减轻 3–4%。值得注意的是，迄今尚无研究证明限时进食能实现具有临床意义的体重减轻（较基线减轻 >5%）。然而，限时进食的文献存在局限，因为尚无研究持续时间超过 12 周。限时进食持续时间更长时，有可能实现具有临床意义的体重减轻。此外，迄今尚无试验直接比较不同形式的间歇性禁食。因此，难以就某一种方案优于另一种方案得出有意义的结论。

> The effects of the three main forms of intermittent fasting on clinical outcome measures are reported in Table 1 (ADF and the 5:2 diet), Table 2 (TRE) and Fig. 2. Studies were included in the assessments in Tables 1 and 2 if they were randomized trials, conducted in adults, had a trial duration of 5 weeks or longer and implemented either a control group (no change in diet or physical activity throughout the study) or a daily calorie restriction group as a comparator in their design. Findings to date reveal that ADF and the 5:2 diet produce similar degrees of weight loss (4–8% loss from baseline) over short intervention periods (8–12 weeks) in men and women with obesity. Longer-​term studies have found that ADF and the 5:2 diet (24–52 weeks) do not result in greater body weight reductions than found in short-​term studies, suggesting that the weight loss efficacy of these diets might peak at 12 weeks. The degree of weight loss achieved with TRE seems to be less pronounced (3–4% loss from baseline) over 8–12 weeks than with ADF and the 5:2 diet. Of note, no study to date has demonstrated clinically significant weight loss (>5% loss from baseline) with TRE. The TRE literature is limited, however, in that no study longer than 12 weeks has been performed. It is possible that clinically significant weight loss would occur with longer durations of TRE. Moreover, no trial to date has directly compared the different forms of intermittent fasting with each other. Thus, it is difficult to draw meaningful conclusions regarding the superiority of one regimen over another.

**2.** 这些研究中评估了若干不同的人群（表1和表2）。隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食对肥胖和超重个体均能有效减重，每周约减重 0.2–0.5 kg，尽管结果存在差异，且只有隔日禁食和5:2 饮食能够产生具有临床意义的减重效果。间歇性禁食对正常体重个体（BMI 18.5–24.9 kg/m）也能促成小幅减重，每周约减重 0.2 kg。间歇性禁食的减重效果似乎不因个体性别或绝经状态而异。存在胰岛素抵抗或糖尿病前期的个体同样能从间歇性禁食干预中获益，且其减重量似乎与无这些状况者相似。仅有兩项试验考察了禁食对1型糖尿病（T1DM）和2型糖尿病（T2DM）患者是否具有减重效果。在接受5:2 饮食52周后，伴有肥胖的T1DM患者体重较基线下降7%。与这些发现相补充的是，伴有肥胖的T2DM患者每周禁食2天、持续52周后体重下降7%。综合来看，这些发现提示间歇性禁食可能对若干不同人群有益，包括肥胖、超重、胰岛素抵抗、糖尿病前期、T1DM和T2DM患者。这些改善是否也见于其他代谢紊乱的患者群体，例如多囊卵巢综合征或甲状腺疾病患者，目前尚不清楚。

> Several different population groups were evaluated in these studies (Tables 1 and 2). ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE were effective for weight loss in individuals with obesity and overweight, and produced approximately 0.2–0.5 kg of weight loss per week, although findings were variable and only ADF and the 5:2 diet were able to produce clinically significant weight loss. Intermittent fasting also facilitated minor reductions in weight in individuals of normal weight (BMI 18.5–24.9 kg/m), inducing approximately 0.2 kg of weight loss per week. The efficacy of intermittent fasting in reducing weight does not seem to vary according to an individual’s sex or menopausal status. Individuals with insulin resistance or prediabetes also benefit from intermittent fasting interventions, and seem to lose similar amounts of weight as those who do not have these conditions. Only two trials have examined if fasting is effective for weight loss in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus (T1DM) and type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). After 52 weeks of the 5:2 diet, patients with T1DM and obesity lost 7% of their body weight from baseline. Complementary to these findings, patients with T2DM and obesity showed 7% weight loss after 52 weeks of fasting 2 days per week. Taken together, these findings suggest that intermittent fasting might benefit several different populations, including those with obesity, overweight, insulin resistance, prediabetes, T1DM and T2DM. Whether these improvements are also noted in patient groups with other metabolic disorders, such as those with polycystic ovary syndrome or thyroid disorders, is not yet known.

**3.** 间歇性禁食在维持体重方面的有效性，目前仅在四项人体试验中接受过检验。这些试验的结果显示，隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食都能有效防止 12–24 周随访期间的体重反弹。这些饮食的体重维持版本与原始方案略有不同。例如，隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食的维持版本允许在禁食日摄入更多能量（每天 1,000–1,200 kcal，而非每天约 500 kcal）。这些调整让人们可以在禁食日稍微多吃一些，从而可能有助于提高这些方案的长期耐受性。至于限时进食，迄今尚无研究考察这种禁食形式能否有助于体重维持。显然，在就这些方案能否帮助人们长期管理体重得出任何可靠结论之前，还需要多得多的数据。

> The efficacy of intermittent fasting in maintaining weight has only been tested in four trials in humans. The results of these trials revealed that both ADF and the 5:2 diet are effective in preventing weight regain during 12–24 weeks of follow-​up. The weight maintenance versions of these diets differ slightly from the original regimens. For instance, the ADF and the 5:2 maintenance diets permit greater energy intake on fast days (1,000–1,200 kcal per day instead of approximately 500 kcal per day). These modifications could help to improve the long-​term tolerability of these regimens by giving people the freedom to eat slightly more on fast days. As for TRE, no study to date has examined whether this form of fasting can facilitate weight maintenance. Evidently, much more data is required before any solid conclusions can be reached regarding the efficacy of these regimens in helping people to manage their weight long-​term.

**4.** 间歇性禁食与传统节食（每日热量限制）对体重的影响，已在多项试验和荟萃分析中进行了研究。在 2017 年的一项研究中，隔日禁食与热量限制（每日热量限制 25%，约每日 1,500 kcal）在超重或肥胖成人中经过 52 周产生了相似的减重程度（较基线减轻 5–6%）。隔日禁食的短期研究（持续时间少于 12 周）也显示出与热量限制相似的体重下降。至于 5:2 饮食，在短期和长期试验中将其与热量限制进行比较时，已报告了相似的减重程度。在 T1DM 或 T2DM 患者中，5:2 饮食与热量限制在 52 周内产生了几乎同等的减重程度（两组较基线均减轻 4–7%）。限时进食是否也能产生与热量限制相似的体重下降，目前仍不清楚，因为迄今为止尚无研究直接比较这两种干预措施。尽管数据有限，这些初步发现表明，间歇性禁食与传统节食在超重、肥胖、T1DM 和 T2DM 个体中产生相似的体重下降。

> The effects of intermittent fasting compared with traditional dieting (daily calorie restriction) on body weight have been investigated in several trials and meta-​analyses. In a 2017 study, ADF and calorie restriction (25% daily calorie restriction, approximately 1,500 kcal per day) produced similar degrees of weight loss (5–6% loss from baseline) over 52 weeks in adults with overweight or obesity. Short-​term studies (less than 12 weeks duration) of ADF have also shown similar body weight reductions to calorie restriction. As for the 5:2 diet, similar degrees of weight loss have been reported when this diet is compared with calorie restriction in short-​term and long-​term trials. In patients with T1DM or T2DM, the 5:2 diet and calorie restriction produced nearly equivalent degrees of weight loss (4–7% loss from baseline in both groups) over 52 weeks. Whether TRE also results in similar weight reductions versus calorie restriction remains unknown, as no study to date has directly compared these two interventions. Although the data are limited, these preliminary findings indicate that intermittent fasting and traditional dieting produce similar reductions in body weight in individuals with overweight, obesity, T1DM and T2DM.

**5.** 当因摄入热量限制饮食而使体重下降时，所减去的体重中约 75% 是脂肪量，25% 是瘦体重。根据此处综述的证据，评估隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食的大多数研究都发现，脂肪量与瘦体重减少的比例（75% 对 25%）与热量限制相似。因此，间歇性禁食很可能无助于肥胖个体在减重期间比传统节食减去更多脂肪量或保留更多瘦体重。间歇性禁食结合运动对身体成分的影响也已有评估。当耐力训练与隔日禁食结合时，瘦体重的变化与单独隔日禁食并无差异。然而，当限时进食与抗阻训练结合时，一些以规律运动（每周进行数次抗阻训练）的瘦个体为对象、且在研究开始前即已如此的研究发现，肌肉量得以维持或增加，同时伴有统计学显著的皮下脂肪量减少。抗阻训练能否帮助肥胖个体在禁食期间保留瘦体重，这一问题颇受关注，但尚待检验。

> When body weight is reduced as a result of consuming a calorie-​restricted diet, approximately 75% of the weight lost is fat mass, and 25% is lean mass. Based on the evidence reviewed here, the majority of studies evaluating ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE found a similar ratio of fat mass to lean mass loss (75% to 25%) as calorie restriction. Thus, it is probable that intermittent fasting does not help individuals with obesity to lose more fat mass or retain more lean mass during weight loss than traditional dieting. The effects of intermittent fasting combined with exercise on body composition have also been evaluated. When endurance training was combined with ADF, changes in lean mass did not differ from that of ADF alone. However, when TRE was combined with resistance training, some studies in lean individuals who exercised regularly (resistance training several times per week) before the start of the study found muscle mass maintenance or accretion, accompanied by statistically significant subcutaneous fat mass loss. Whether resistance training can help individuals with obesity to preserve lean mass during periods of fasting is of interest but has yet to be tested.

**6.** 内脏脂肪质量的变化也已被研究。在大多数评估隔日禁食和5:2 饮食并发现具有临床意义体重减轻的试验中，与无干预对照组（研究期间饮食或体力活动无变化）相比，接受饮食干预者的内脏脂肪质量出现了统计学显著下降。相比之下，限时进食通常未观察到内脏脂肪质量的减少，但这可能是因为在这些短期试验（持续时间少于 12 周）中，该干预所实现的体重减轻幅度有限（较基线减轻 3–4%）。当直接比较热量限制与隔日禁食或5:2 饮食之间内脏脂肪质量的变化时，未观察到统计学显著差异。这一发现提示，当实现同等体重减轻时，每日热量限制与间歇性禁食对内脏脂肪质量产生相似的减少效果。

> Changes in visceral fat mass have also been examined. In the majority of trials evaluating ADF and the 5:2 diet that found clinically significant weight loss, visceral fat mass decreased statistically significantly in those receiving the dietary interventions compared with no-​intervention controls (no change in diet or physical activity during the study). By contrast, reductions in visceral fat mass were not generally observed with TRE, but this could have been due to the limited amount of weight loss (3–4% loss from baseline) achieved with this intervention in these short-​term trials (less than 12 weeks duration). When changes in visceral fat mass were directly compared between calorie restriction and ADF or the 5:2 diet, no statistically significant differences were observed. This finding would suggest that both daily energy restriction and intermittent fasting produce similar reductions in visceral fat mass when equivalent weight loss is achieved.


#### Effects of intermittent fasting on energy intake.（p3）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/energy-intake-and-diet-quality/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-energ#01 -->
**1.** 个体通过间歇性禁食减轻体重的主要原因在于，这些干预措施有助于人们减少能量摄入。人体试验的结果表明，相对于基线水平，隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食可使总体能量摄入减少约 10–30%。临床医生常常担心，采用隔日禁食或 5:2 饮食的患者会在进食日过度进食。然而，越来越多的证据表明这种情况并不会发生。受试者在进食日摄入的能量通常仅比其计算出的能量需求多出 10–15%（约 200–300 kcal）。事实上，一项研究发现，采用 5:2 饮食的个体在进食日的能量摄入比研究开始前还有所减少。由于受试者并不会通过在进食日吃得更多来完全补偿禁食日未摄入的食物，因此产生了净能量亏空，从而导致轻度至中度的体重减轻。

> The main reason why individuals lose weight with intermittent fasting is because these interventions help people to reduce energy intake. Findings from trials in humans indicate that ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE reduce overall energy intake by approximately 10–30% relative to baseline. Clinicians are often worried that patients on ADF or the 5:2 diet will overeat on feast days. However, accumulating evidence suggests that this does not occur. Participants typically only consume an extra 10–15% of their energy needs (approximately 200–300 kcal) on feast days relative to their calculated energy needs. Indeed, one study found reduced energy intake on feast days in individuals on the 5:2 diet compared with energy intake before the start of the study. As participants do not fully compensate for lack of food consumed on the fast day by eating more on the feast day, a net energy deficit is created, resulting in mild to moderate weight loss.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/energy-intake-and-diet-quality/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-energ#02 -->
**2.** 临床医生也对患者在「限时进食」的进食窗口内过度进食表示担忧。与可能预期的情况相反，大多数个体无法在规定 4 至 8 小时的进食窗口内摄入他们此前所吃的食物量。因此，「限时进食」会导致每日能量摄入出现计划外的减少，幅度在 10–30% 之间。「限时进食」还有一个额外的好处，即不要求个体为了减重而计算卡路里。人们退出「隔日禁食」和热量限制试验的主要原因之一，是他们厌倦了必须定期警惕地监测自己的食物摄入。「限时进食」饮食能够避免这一要求，因为它让参与者只需“看钟表”，而不必监测食物摄入，同时仍能产生轻度减重。遗憾的是，迄今为止尚无研究表明「限时进食」能带来具有临床意义的减重。在更长的干预周期（>24 周）内，「限时进食」期间观察到的能量摄入减少是否会持续，以及这些能量摄入的减少是否会转化为具有临床意义的减重，将是有趣的问题。

> Clinicians have also expressed concern over patients overeating during the eating window of TRE. Contrary to what might be expected, most individuals are not able to consume the amount of food they previously ate within the prescribed 4-​h to 8-​h eating window. As such, TRE results in an unplanned reduction in daily energy intake, in the range of 10–30%. TRE also has the added benefit of not requiring individuals to count calories in order to lose weight. One of the main reasons why people drop out of ADF and calorie restriction trials is because they grow tired of having to vigilantly monitor their food intake on a regular basis. TRE diets are able to avoid this requirement by allowing participants to simply ‘watch the clock’ instead of monitoring food intake, while still producing mild weight loss. Unfortunately, no study to date has shown clinically significant weight loss with TRE. It will be interesting to see if the observed reductions in energy intake during TRE persist during longer intervention periods (>24 weeks) and if these reductions in energy intake translate into clinically significant weight loss.


#### Effects of intermittent fasting on diet quality.（p5）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/energy-intake-and-diet-quality/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-diet-#01 -->
**1.** 由于间歇性禁食允许在一周中的某些天或一天中的某些时间窗口内自由进食，营养师和医生对这些进食模式对饮食质量的影响表示担忧。这些担忧包括：个体在采用间歇性禁食饮食期间可能会减少水果和蔬菜的摄入，转而食用更多能量密集和高脂肪的食物。已有八项试验评估了宏量营养素和饮料摄入的变化。在这些试验中，与基线水平相比，采用隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食的参与者在试验过程中膳食脂肪、碳水化合物和蛋白质摄入的比例没有变化。此外，与基线摄入量相比，采用这些方案的参与者在糖、饱和脂肪、单不饱和脂肪、多不饱和脂肪、胆固醇和钠的摄入水平上没有统计学显著差异。大多数试验发现，在研究开始时纤维摄入水平较低（约每天 10–15 g）。在采用间歇性禁食或热量限制饮食的人群中，纤维摄入量在研究过程中通常不会发生变化，即使提供了增加水果、蔬菜和全谷物摄入的饮食咨询也是如此。咖啡、茶、无糖汽水、能量饮料和酒精等饮料的摄入在间歇性禁食期间也保持不变。间歇性禁食饮食对维生素和矿物质摄入的影响尚不清楚，因为尚无研究直接评估禁食期间微量营养素摄入的变化。然而，由于目前尚不清楚长期禁食是否会导致关键微量营养素（如维生素 D、维生素 B12 和电解质等）摄入量降低，应常规评估这些维生素和矿物质的循环水平以监测缺乏情况。综合这些发现，禁食似乎对饮食质量没有任何有益或有害的影响。然而，应当指出，这些研究中有许多建议参与者在自由进食期间注意自己的饮食习惯，这可能混淆了膳食摄入数据。

> As intermittent fasting permits ad libitum food intake on certain days of the week, or during certain time windows in the day, dieticians and physicians have expressed concerns regarding the effects of these eating patterns on diet quality. These concerns include worries that individuals would lower their fruit and vegetable intake and eat more energy-​dense and high-​fat foods while on intermittent fasting diets. Changes in macronutrient and beverage intake have been assessed in eight trials. In these trials, the proportions of dietary fat, carbohydrates and protein intake did not change over the course of the trials in participants on ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE compared with baseline levels. Moreover, levels of sugar, saturated fat, monounsaturated fat, polyunsaturated fat, cholesterol and sodium intake did not differ statistically significantly in participants on these regimens compared from baseline intake. Most trials found low levels of fibre intake (approximately 10–15 g per day) at the beginning of the study. Fibre consumption generally does not change over the course of the study in people on intermittent fasting or calorie-​restriction diets, even when dietary counselling to increase fruit, vegetable and whole grain intake is provided. Intake of beverages, such as coffee, tea, diet sodas, energy drinks and alcohol, also remains unchanged during intermittent fasting. The effect of intermittent fasting diets on vitamin and mineral intake is not yet known, as no study has directly evaluated changes in micronutrient consumption during fasting. However, as it is currently unclear whether long-​term fasting could result in lower intakes of key micronutrients, such as vitamin D, vitamin B12 and electrolytes, amongst others, circulating levels of these vitamins and minerals should be routinely assessed to monitor for deficiencies. Taking these findings together, fasting does not seem to have any beneficial or detrimental effects on diet quality. However, it should be noted that many of these studies advised participants to be mindful of their eating habits during periods of ad libitum intake, which might have confounded the dietary intake data.


#### Effects of intermittent fasting on blood pressure.（p7）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#01 -->
**1.** 隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食对血压的影响差异很大。虽然有几项研究显示收缩压（较基线下降 3–11%）和舒张压（较基线下降 3–13%）降低，但另有多项研究显示没有效果。不过，那些显示出改善的试验，其受试者似乎大多在基线时血压偏高。因此，间歇性禁食可能只在治疗开始时患有高血压或临界高血压的人群中才有助降低血压。基于迄今的研究发现，这三种间歇性禁食形式对血压的降低幅度似乎相似，各饮食方案之间没有明显差异。

> The effects of ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE on blood pressure are highly variable. While several studies have demonstrated reductions in systolic blood pressure (3–11% decrease from baseline) and diastolic blood pressure (3–13% decrease from baseline), several others have shown no effect. However, it would seem that many of the trials that showed improvements involved participants with elevated blood pressure at baseline. Thus, it is possible that intermittent fasting might only help to lower blood pressure in people with hypertension or borderline hypertension at the start of treatment. Based on findings to date, the three forms of intermittent fasting seem to produce similar reductions in blood pressure, with no apparent deviations between diets.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#02 -->
**2.** 对于所有三种形式的间歇性禁食，更大程度的体重减轻似乎并未比更低程度的体重减轻产生更明显的血压降低。研究还注意到，在早期限时进食（即在15:00 h之前摄入所有食物）的情况下，血压在体重未减轻的情况下即出现下降。这些发现提示，血压的改善可能部分归因于禁食本身（即归因于长时间不摄入食物），而不仅仅是归因于体重减轻。关于禁食为何可能在体重未减轻的情况下改善血压，其生理学解释仍不清楚，但可能涉及传出交感神经活动和静脉血浆去甲肾上腺素水平的轻度升高。还有一种可能是，早期限时进食可能通过将盐摄入移至一天中较早的时段来促进尿钠排泄（即盐随尿液排出），因为在该时段钠排泄受昼夜节律系统上调。

> Greater degrees of weight loss did not seem to produce more pronounced reductions in blood pressure than lower levels of weight loss for all three forms of intermittent fasting. It was also noted that with early TRE (eating all food before 15:00 h), blood pressure was reduced in the absence of body weight reductions. These findings suggest that the improvements in blood pressure could be partially due to the fasting itself (that is, due to long periods of food intake abstention), and not just due to the weight loss. The physiological explanation as to why fasting might improve blood pressure in the absence of weight loss is still unclear, but might involve mild increases in efferent sympathetic nerve activity and venous plasma levels of noradrenaline. It is also possible that early TRE might promote natri uresis (that is, salt excretion in the urine) by shifting salt intake to earlier in the day, when sodium excretion is upregulated by the circadian system.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#03 -->
**3.** 在评估间歇性禁食的试验中，通常会检测血浆脂质浓度的变化。在三项间歇性禁食试验中，LDL 胆固醇水平较基线下降 10–22%，但大多数研究显示无变化。甘油三酯浓度也较基线下降 16–36%，但这一下降在不同研究之间并不一致。当直接比较间歇性禁食与热量限制时，观察到 LDL 胆固醇和甘油三酯水平的下降幅度相似，提示这两种干预对这些脂质参数的影响相当。由于迄今研究数量有限，难以确定脂质降低的程度是否因患者的 BMI 分类、干预持续时间、患者基线脂质水平或患者实现的体重减轻量而有所不同。同样难以确定某种形式的间歇性禁食是否比另一种能更大程度地降低 LDL 胆固醇或甘油三酯水平。一项直接比较隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食对代谢变量影响的研究，将有助于阐明在改变这些危险因素方面，是否有一种饮食优于另一种。

> Changes in plasma concentrations of lipids are routinely assessed in trials evaluating intermittent fasting. Levels of LDL cholesterol decreased by 10–22% from baseline in three trials of intermittent fasting, but showed no change in the majority of studies. Triglyceride concentrations also decreased by 16–36% from baseline, but this decrease was not consistent between studies. When direct comparisons were made between intermittent fasting and calorie restriction, similar decreases in levels of LDL cholesterol and triglycerides were noted, suggesting that these two interventions have equivalent effects on these lipid parameters. Due to the limited number of studies to date, it is difficult to determine whether the degree of lipid reduction varies by the patient’s BMI category, the intervention duration, the patient’s baseline lipid levels or the amount of weight loss the patient achieves. It is also difficult to ascertain whether one form of intermittent fasting produces greater reductions in LDL cholesterol or triglyceride levels compared with another. A study that directly compares the effects of ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE on metabolic variables would be instrumental in helping to clarify whether one diet is superior to another in altering these risk factors.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#04 -->
**4.** 相比之下，高密度脂蛋白（HDL）胆固醇的水平通常保持不变，或在间歇性禁食下略有下降。这一发现有些令人意外，因为在急性减重期间，由于卵磷脂胆固醇酯转移蛋白减少，HDL胆固醇水平通常会降低。因此，在体重维持期间，HDL胆固醇往往会回升至基线水平。评估间歇性禁食的试验中为何未观察到这一模式，目前尚不清楚，但可能与此类试验在急性减重阶段实现的减重幅度较小有关。唯一一项显示HDL胆固醇明显升高的研究，是2013年一项将隔日禁食与耐力运动相结合的研究。由于耐力训练已被证明可提高HDL胆固醇水平，这一效应很可能源于运动部分，而非禁食干预。

> Levels of HDL cholesterol, by contrast, generally remain unchanged or slightly decrease with intermittent fasting. This finding is somewhat surprising as levels of HDL cholesterol typically reduce during periods of acute weight loss due to reductions in lecithin cholesterol ester transfer protein. Consequently, during periods of weight maintenance, HDL cholesterol tends to rebound to baseline levels. The reason why this pattern was not noted in trials evaluating intermittent fasting is unclear, but could be related to the low amount of weight loss achieved during the acute weight loss phase in these trials. The only study that demonstrated distinct increases in HDL cholesterol was a 2013 study that combined ADF with endurance exercise. As endurance training has been shown to augment levels of HDL cholesterol, this effect was most probably due to the exercise component, rather than the fasting intervention.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#05 -->
**5.** 本综述所讨论证据的一个主要局限在于，间歇性禁食研究中采血时间不明确。在大多数研究中，血液采集是在禁食日还是进食日进行并未说明。采血检测前的禁食时长会对血浆脂质水平产生相当大的影响。例如，正如一项关于早期限时进食的研究所指出的，较长的急性禁食时长（>18 h）可通过增强脂解作用导致循环中甘油三酯和游离脂肪酸浓度升高。因此，未来研究有必要阐明脂质及其他代谢风险因素水平的变化如何随该饮食的两个不同阶段（即禁食与进食）而变化。

> A major limitation to the evidence discussed in this Review is the lack of clarity regarding the timing of the blood samples taken throughout the intermittent fasting studies. In the majority of studies, whether the blood draws were performed on fast days or feast days was not specified. The duration of fasting before blood was taken for testing would have considerable effects on plasma levels of lipids. For instance, longer acute fasting durations (>18 h) can lead to elevated circulating concentrations of triglycerides and free fatty acids through augmented lipolysis, as noted in one study of early TRE. It will therefore be important for future research to clarify how changes in the levels of lipids and other metabolic risk factors vary according to these two distinct phases of the diet, namely, fasting versus feasting.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#06 -->
**6.** 间歇性禁食调节血糖参数的能力，在间歇性禁食研究中也被常规评估。在隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食期间，空腹葡萄糖浓度通常保持不变。然而，这些研究大多纳入的是基线时未患 T1DM 或 T2DM 的个体。在未患糖尿病的参与者中，空腹葡萄糖水平通常控制良好，因此这一发现并不令人意外。另一方面，在若干试验中，空腹胰岛素水平较基线降低了 11–38%。此外，与基线胰岛素水平处于正常范围的个体相比，这一效应在基线胰岛素水平升高（>13 μIU/ml）的个体中更常被观察到。间歇性禁食饮食对胰岛素抵抗和胰岛素敏感性的影响差异很大，一些研究显示有改善，但大多数研究显示无影响。关于 HbA1c，大多数研究显示，在未患 T1DM 或 T2DM 的个体中，该参数没有变化。

> The ability of intermittent fasting to modulate glycaemic parameters is also routinely assessed in studies of intermittent fasting. Fasting concentrations of glucose generally remain unchanged during ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE. However, the majority of these studies involved individuals who did not have T1DM or T2DM at baseline. Fasting levels of glucose are typically well controlled in participants who do not have diabetes mellitus, so this finding is not surprising. Fasting levels of insulin, on the other hand, were reduced by 11–38% from baseline in several trials. Furthermore, this effect was observed more frequently in individuals with elevated levels of insulin at baseline (>13 μIU/ml) compared with individuals with baseline levels of insulin in the normal range. The effects of intermittent fasting diets on insulin resistance and insulin sensitivity are highly variable, with some studies showing improvements, but most showing no effect. Regarding HbA1c, the majority of studies show no change in this parameter in individuals who do not have either T1DM or T2DM.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-blood#07 -->
**7.** 很少有研究考察禁食如何影响糖尿病患者的血糖控制。在 T2DM 患者中，5:2 饮食在治疗 52 周后使体重（7%）和 HbA1c（0.5%）显著下降。这些有益变化与热量限制相似，后者使体重下降 5%、HbA1c 降低 0.3%；在 5:2 饮食组和热量限制组中，口服降糖药和胰岛素的用量随时间显著减少，且与体重变化相关。相比之下，在 T1DM 合并肥胖的患者中，5:2 饮食使体重下降 7%，但 52 周后对 HbA1c 水平没有影响。这两项试验均表明，每周禁食 2 天对 T1DM 和 T2DM 患者是安全的，且与很少的低血糖事件相关。尽管如此，仍需要在糖尿病人群中开展更多研究，以阐明这些饮食在该人群中的安全性和有效性。

> Very few studies have examined how fasting affects glycaemic control in individuals with diabetes mellitus. In patients with T2DM, the 5:2 diet produced notable reductions in body weight (7%) and HbA1c (0.5%) after 52 weeks of treatment. These beneficial changes were similar to those of calorie restriction, which produced 5% weight loss and a 0.3% reduction in HbA1c of oral hypoglycaemic agents and insulin decreased significantly over time in both the 5:2 diet group and the calorie restriction group, and was correlated with weight change. By contrast, in patients with T1DM and obesity, the 5:2 diet resulted in a 7% weight loss, but had no effect on HbA1c levels after 52 weeks. Both of these trials demonstrated that fasting 2 days per week is safe in patients with T1DM and T2DM and is associated with few hypo glycaemic events. Nonetheless, much more research in people with diabetes mellitus is needed to clarify the safety and efficacy of these diets in this population group.


#### Effects of intermittent fasting on inflammation and oxidative stress.（p8）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/metabolic-disease-risk-factors/effects-of-intermittent-fasting-on-infla#01 -->
**1.** 炎症和氧化应激在心血管疾病和 T2DM 的发生与进展中具有不可或缺的作用。禁食对关键炎症标志物（如 TNF、IL-6、同型半胱氨酸和 C 反应蛋白）的影响，已在七项人体试验中进行了检验。这些研究的结果表明，隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和 限时进食 通常对这些循环炎症标志物没有影响。相比之下，间歇性禁食已被反复证明能够降低氧化应激标志物。例如，在遵循间歇性禁食饮食 8–24 周后，8-异前列腺素（脂质氧化降解的一种标志物）、速效晚期氧化蛋白产物、硝基酪氨酸和蛋白质羰基的水平已被证明相当一致地下降。这些氧化应激标志物的下降，可能归因于禁食带来的胰岛素敏感性改善。在氧化条件下，胰岛素信号传导受损，进而导致细胞胰岛素抵抗。此外，与接受安慰剂的超重个体相比，接受抗氧化剂（如维生素 E）后其胰岛素敏感性有所改善。因此，间歇性禁食可能改善胰岛素敏感性的机制之一，就是通过降低氧化应激。

> Inflammation and oxidative stress have an integral role in the development and progression of cardiovascular disease and T2DM. The effects of fasting on key markers of inflammation, such as TNF, IL-6, homocysteine and C-​reactive protein, have been examined in seven trials in humans. The results of these studies indicate that ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE generally have no effect on these circulating inflammatory markers. By contrast, intermittent fasting has been routinely demonstrated to reduce markers of oxidative stress. For instance, levels of 8-​isoprostane (a marker of oxidative degradation of lipids), fast-​acting advanced oxidation protein products, nitrotyrosine and protein carbonyls have been shown to decrease fairly consistently after 8–24 weeks of following an intermittent fasting diet. It is possible that these decreases in markers of oxidative stress are associated with improvements in insulin sensitivity due to fasting. Insulin signalling is impaired under oxidative conditions, which leads to cellular insulin resistance. Moreover, insulin sensitivity is improved in individuals with overweight following administration of antioxidants, such as vitamin E, compared with insulin sensitivity in those receiving placebo. Therefore, one of the mechanisms by which intermittent fasting might improve insulin sensitivity is by decreasing oxidative stress.


### Safety implications（p8）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/safety-implications#01 -->
**1.** 临床试验的证据表明，隔日禁食和限时进食不会导致便秘、腹泻、恶心、口干、口臭、易怒、疲劳或头晕的发生频率增加。在禁食的头2周内，偶尔会有人报告因脱水而出现头痛，但通过充分饮水即可纠正。

> Evidence from clinical trials suggests that ADF and TRE do not result in an increased frequency of constipation, diarrhoea, nausea, dry mouth, halitosis, irritability, fatigue or dizziness. Headaches are occasionally reported during the first 2 weeks of fasting due to dehydration, but these can be corrected with sufficient water intake.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/safety-implications#02 -->
**2.** 有人推测间歇性禁食可能增加发生进食障碍的风险。一项2015年评估隔日禁食的试验和一项2019年评估限时进食的试验的数据显示，禁食并未提高暴食、清除行为、抑郁或害怕超重的发生率。然而，这些试验排除了有进食障碍史的参与者。因此，对于已确诊进食障碍者，禁食是否安全仍属未知。此外，对于有发生进食障碍风险的个体——例如自尊心低、身体意象差、负性情感（一种涉及体验负性情绪和自我概念差的人格变量）、进食行为紊乱或内化体重偏见者——这些方案是否安全也仍不确定。鉴于该领域缺乏安全性数据，临床医生在向可能有发生进食障碍风险的个体推荐禁食方法时应谨慎行事。

> It has been speculated that intermittent fasting could increase the risk of developing an eating disorder. Data from a 2015 trial evaluating ADF and a 2019 trial evaluating TRE show that fasting does not increase the rates of binge eating, purgative behaviour, depression or fear of becoming overweight. However, participants with a history of an eating disorder were excluded from these trials. Thus, it remains unknown whether fasting is safe in those with a diagnosed eating disorder. Moreover, whether these regimens are safe in individuals at risk of developing an eating disorder, such as those with low self-esteem, poor body image, negative affect (a personality variable that involves the experience of nega22. The use tive emotions and poor self-​concept), disordered eating behaviours or internalized weight bias is still uncertain. In view of the lack of safety data in this area, clinicians should exercise caution when recommending fasting approaches to individuals who might be at risk of developing an eating disorder.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/safety-implications#03 -->
**3.** 迄今为止，只有两项试验评估了禁食期间生殖激素水平的变化。在绝经前女性中，采用 5:2 饮食 24 周后，循环中雄烯二酮、睾酮、硫酸脱氢表雄酮、性激素结合球蛋白和催乳素的浓度保持不变。在年轻、瘦削的男性中，8 周的限时进食降低了游离睾酮和总睾酮浓度，但这些降低与瘦体重或肌肉力量的有害变化无关。禁食对生育力的影响仍属未知，因为迄今尚未开展任何试验。至于甲状腺激素水平，在健康个体和亚临床甲状腺功能减退症患者中，循环中游离四碘甲状腺原氨酸、三碘甲状腺原氨酸和促甲状腺激素的水平均保持不变。

> Changes in reproductive hormone levels during fasting have only been assessed in two trials to date. In premenopausal women, circulating concentrations of androstenedione, testosterone, dehydroepiandrosterone sulfate, sex hormone-​binding globulin and prolactin remained unchanged after 24 weeks on the 5:2 diet. In young, lean men, 8 weeks of TRE decreased free and total testosterone concentrations, but these reductions were not related to deleterious changes in lean mass or muscle strength. The effects of fasting on fertility remains unknown, as no trial has been performed to date. As for thyroid hormone levels, circulating levels of free tetraiodothyronine, triiodothyronine and thyroid-stimulating hormone remain unchanged in healthy individuals and in those with subclinical hypothyroidism.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/safety-implications#04 -->
**4.** 也有人推测，间歇性禁食可能对静息代谢率产生不利影响。尽管普通公众普遍持有这种看法，但证据表明，在体重维持不变时，禁食对静息代谢率没有影响；而当体重较基线下降 5–7% 时，静息代谢率仅出现小幅下降（每天 100–200 kcal）。此外，减重期间由间歇性禁食引起的静息代谢率下降，与每日热量限制所引起的下降相似。

> It has also been postulated that intermittent fasting could have deleterious effects on resting metabolic rate. Despite this widely held belief amongst the general public, evidence shows that fasting either has no effect on resting metabolic rate when weight is maintained, or results in minor reductions (100–200 kcal per day) when weight is reduced by 5–7% from baseline. Moreover, these decreases in resting metabolic rate caused by intermittent fasting during weight loss are similar to those caused by daily calorie restriction.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/safety-implications#05 -->
**5.** 综合来看，这些初步发现提示，间歇性禁食产生的胃肠道、神经、激素或代谢方面的不良结局很少。然而，由于在人类禁食试验中很少评估不良事件，目前尚难以就这些饮食的安全性得出明确结论。

> Taken together, these preliminary findings suggest that intermittent fasting produces few gastrointestinal, neurological, hormonal or metabolic adverse outcomes. However, as adverse events are seldom assessed in trials in humans of fasting, definitive conclusions regarding the safety of these diets are difficult to draw at present.


#### Indications and contraindications.（p9）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/practical-considerations/indications-and-contraindicationsu002e#01 -->
**1.** 不应给12岁以下儿童、妊娠期或哺乳期女性开具间歇性禁食，因为迄今为止尚无研究评估这些饮食方案在这些人群中的安全性（框1）。有进食障碍史或BMI低于18.5 kg/m的人不建议禁食。70岁以上的老年人也应被提醒谨慎使用这些饮食方案，因为禁食对衰老诱导的肌少症的影响仍不确定。此外，需要在一天中特定时间随餐服药的患者，可能难以遵循某些限时进食方案。这些患者应与临床医生合作，找到最适合其用药方案的禁食方案。超重或肥胖、不患有T1DM、T2DM或其他合并症的健康成年人，可以在无医学监督的情况下安全地采用这些饮食方案。

> Intermittent fasting should not be prescribed to children under the age of 12 years, or women who are pregnant or lactating, as no study to date has evaluated the safety of these diets in these population groups (Box 1). Fasting is not recommended in people with a history of an eating disorder or a BMI of less than 18.5 kg/m. Elderly individuals over the age of 70 years should also be cautioned against the use of these diets, as the effects of fasting on ageing-​induced sarcopenia remains uncertain. Moreover, patients who need to take medications with meals at certain times of the day might find it difficult to follow certain TRE regimens. These patients should work with their clinicians to find a fasting protocol that works best with their medication regimen. Healthy adults with overweight or obesity, who do not have T1DM, T2DM or other comorbidities, can safely undertake these diets without medical supervision.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/practical-considerations/indications-and-contraindicationsu002e#02 -->
**2.** 过去几年的研究结果表明，禁食可能对肥胖青少年的体重控制有效。然而，只有临床上确有减重指征时，才应在这类人群中推荐禁食，并且必须在医疗监督下进行。对于肥胖青少年禁食的安全性，临床医生和研究人员的看法不一。一些人认为，禁食对这一人群可能过于极端，因为青春期是进食障碍发生的高峰人生阶段。相反，另一些人则指出，有证据表明，在医疗监督下进行减重饮食并不会增加肥胖青少年患进食障碍的风险。要进一步阐明这类饮食对这一人群的安全性，无疑还需要更多研究。

> Findings from the past few years suggest that fasting might be effective for weight control in adolescents with obesity. However, fasting in this group should only be recommended when weight loss is clinically indicated and should only be undertaken under medi cal supervision. Opinions of clinicians and researchers vary regarding the safety of fasting in adolescents with obesity. Some feel that fasting might be too extreme in this population, as adolescence is the peak life stage for eating disorder development. By contrast, others point to evidence suggesting that medically supervised weight loss diets do not increase the risk of an eating disorder in adolescents with obesity. More research is undoubtedly needed to further elucidate the safety of these diets in this group.


#### Advice when starting, following and stopping intermittent fasting.（p9）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/practical-considerations/advice-when-startingu002c-following-and-#01 -->
**1.** 与预期相反，间歇性禁食通常并不难融入日常生活。然而，临床医生应当注意，采用这些饮食方案中的任何一种，都会有一到两周的适应期（框2）。在这一初始阶段，头痛是常见的报告症状，但当饮水量增加到每天1.5升时，头痛往往会缓解。一旦患者适应了其禁食方案，他们就可以进行正常的运动锻炼，即使在禁食期间也是如此。过去几年的试验表明，受试者能够在12小时至36小时的禁食期间进行中等强度至高强度的耐力或抗阻运动，且不会出现任何不良影响。

> Contrary to what would be expected, intermittent fasting is generally not difficult to incorporate into day-​to-​day life. Clinicians should be aware, however, that there is an adjustment period of 1 to 2 weeks with any of these diets (Box 2). Headaches are commonly reported during this initial period, but these tend to subside when water intake is increased to 1.5 l per day. Once patients have adapted to their fasting protocol, they can engage in their normal exercise routine, even during periods of fasting. Trials from the past few years show that participants are able to do moderate-​ intensity to high-​intensity endurance or resistance exercise during 12-​h to 36-​h periods of food abstention without any adverse effects.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/practical-considerations/advice-when-startingu002c-following-and-#02 -->
**2.** 关于间歇性禁食期间食用的食物类型，应鼓励患者多摄入水果、蔬菜和全谷物，以增加膳食纤维和微量营养素的摄入。此外，在隔日禁食和 5:2 饮食的禁食日，建议至少摄入 50 g 瘦肉蛋白，以帮助控制饥饿感并防止瘦体重过度流失。允许饮酒，但不建议在隔日禁食或 5:2 饮食的禁食日饮酒，因为这些天的能量摄入已被限制在约 500 kcal。在禁食窗口期内，允许饮用含咖啡因的饮料（如能量饮料，或不加糖、牛奶或奶油的咖啡和茶）以及无糖汽水。然而，无糖汽水的摄入应限制为每天两份，因为这类饮料可能增加对糖的渴望。

> As for the types of foods consumed during intermittent fasting, patients should be encouraged to consume plenty of fruits, vegetables and whole grains to boost their fibre and micronutrient intake. It is also advisable to consume at least 50 g of lean protein on the fast day of ADF and the 5:2 diet to help to control hunger and prevent excessive lean mass loss. Alcohol is permitted, but it is not recommended on the fast day of ADF or the 5:2 diet, as energy intake is already limited on these days to approximately 500 kcal. Caffeinated bever ages (such as energy drinks or coffee and tea without sugar, milk or cream) and diet sodas are allowed during the fasting window. However, diet soda intake should be limited to two servings per day as these beverages can increase sugar craving.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/practical-considerations/advice-when-startingu002c-following-and-#03 -->
**3.** 行为改变策略应作为禁食的辅助疗法加以鼓励，以帮助促进体重管理。结构化的行为改变方案通过教会患者如何定期自我监测其食物摄入、活动水平和体重，帮助他们实现减重目标。行为治疗可由经过培训的干预人员在小组或一对一环境中提供。此外，行为改变方案也可在线获取，并以移动健康应用程序的形式提供。

> Behavioural change strategies should be encouraged as an adjunct therapy to fasting to help promote weight management. Structured behavioural change programmes help patients achieve their weight loss goals by teaching them how to regularly self-​monitor their food intake, activity levels and body weight. Behavioural therapy can be provided by a trained interventionist in a group or one-​on-​one setting. Alternatively, behavioural change programmes are also available online and as mobile health apps.


### Considerations for diabetes mellitus（p9）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/considerations-for-diabetes-mellitus#01 -->
**1.** 尽管证据仍然非常有限，但初步研究结果表明，间歇性禁食对T1DM和T2DM患者可能是安全的。然而，糖尿病患者在间歇性禁食期间需要由医生密切监测。研究表明，在适当的医疗管理到位的情况下，这些患者发生低血糖和高血糖的情况很少见。

> Although the evidence is still very limited, preliminary findings suggest that intermittent fasting might be safe in patients with T1DM and T2DM. However, patients with diabetes mellitus need to be monitored closely by their physician during periods of intermittent fasting. Studies show that occurrences of hypoglycaemia and hyperglycaemia are rare in these patients when appropriate medical management is in place.


#### Reviews Blood glucose monitoring.（p10）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/considerations-for-diabetes-mellitus/reviews-blood-glucose-monitoringu002e#01 -->
**1.** 建议T1DM或T2DM患者在间歇性禁食期间全天定期检测并记录空腹血糖水平（例如每餐前、餐后2 h以及睡前）。在积极减重阶段（通常为饮食方案的前3个月），患者应每周与医生联系，以监测不良反应并调整用药。如果连续两次血糖水平低于70 mg/dl，患者应联系医生调整用药。如果血糖水平高于180 mg/dl，应核查患者对禁食方案的饮食依从性，必要时调整用药。

> It is recommended that patients with T1DM or T2DM test and record their fasting blood levels of glucose regularly throughout the day during intermittent fasting (for example, immediately before and 2 h after each meal, and before sleeping). Patients should be in weekly contact with their physician during the active weight loss period (typically the first 3 months of the diet) to monitor adverse effects and adjust medications. If their blood levels of glucose are less than 70 mg/dl on two consecutive occasions, patients should contact their physician for medication changes. If blood levels of glucose are higher than 180 mg/dl, dietary compliance with the fasting protocol should be checked and changes to medications should be made if necessary.


#### Medication management in patients with T1DM.（p10）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/considerations-for-diabetes-mellitus/medication-management-in-patients-with-t#01 -->
**1.** 根据现有文献，接受隔日禁食或 5:2 饮食的 T1DM 患者，可建议在禁食日将基础胰岛素剂量减少 50%，在非禁食日减少 10%。在积极减重期间，胰岛素剂量应每周调整一次，依据是前 7 天测得的最低餐前血糖水平。限时进食期间的药物管理方案仍属未知，因为尚无研究在 T1DM 患者中开展。

> Based on the available literature, patients with T1DM who are on ADF or the 5:2 diet might be advised to reduce their basal insulin dose by 50% on fast days and by 10% on non-​fast days. Insulin doses should be adjusted weekly during active weight loss, based on the lowest pre-​meal blood level of glucose measured in the preceding 7 days. A medication management protocol during TRE is still unknown, as no study has been performed in patients with T1DM.


#### Medication management in patients with T2DM.（p10）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/considerations-for-diabetes-mellitus/medication-management-in-patients-with-t-2#01 -->
**1.** 根据现有文献，对于 HbA1c 水平 <7% 的 T2DM 患者，可建议其在「隔日禁食」和「5:2 饮食」期间停用磺脲类药物和胰岛素。然而，如果患者在开始饮食时 HbA1c 水平介于 7% 至 10% 之间，则可在禁食日当天停用磺脲类药物和胰岛素，长效胰岛素可在禁食日前一晚停用。如果开始「隔日禁食」或「5:2 饮食」时 HbA1c 水平 >10%，磺脲类药物可保持不变，但长效胰岛素仅可在禁食日减少约 10 个单位。内分泌科医生应与每位患者逐一协作，以确保最佳诊疗；需注意，应告知患者未经医嘱不得自行调整用药。T2DM 患者在「限时进食」期间的用药方案尚未明确，因为目前尚无相关数据。此外，目前对于较新型药物（如 SGLT2 抑制剂和 GLP1 受体激动剂）的管理也没有明确的方案，因为目前尚无这些药物在禁食期间的安全性或在有效性数据发表。

> Based on the available literature, patients with T2DM with an HbA1c level of <7% might be advised to discontinue sulfonylureas and insulin during ADF and the 5:2 diet. However, if the patient’s HbA1c level is between 7% and 10% at the start of the diet, sulfonylureas and insulin could be discontinued on the fast day only, and long-​acting insulin could be discontinued the night before a fast day. If the HbA1c level is >10% when starting ADF or 5:2, sulfonylurea medications could remain unchanged, but long-​acting insulin could be decreased by approximately 10 units on fast days only. An endocrinologist should work with each patient individually to ensure the best care; note that patients should be advised not to adjust their medication without medical advice. A medication protocol during TRE in patients with T2DM has yet to be clarified as no data are available. Moreover, there is currently no clear protocol for managing newer agents, such as SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP1 receptor agonists, as there are no published safety or efficacy data available for these medications during fasting at present.


### Future directions（p10）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/future-directions#01 -->
**1.** 截至 2010 年，仅有四项单臂试验评估了间歇性禁食对人类受试者健康相关结局的影响。过去 10 年间，人们对这些饮食方案的关注度大幅上升，关于间歇性禁食饮食效果的数据每个月都在不断涌现。尽管过去几年数据大量涌入，但要想加深对该领域的理解，仍有许多工作要做（专栏 3）。具体而言，亟需长期（>1 年）临床试验。迄今为止，该领域的绝大多数研究持续时间都很短（4 至 12 周），仅有三项研究考察了长期效果（52 周）。持续多年的随机对照试验和定性研究将为了解这些方案的安全性、可行性以及长期体重和心脏代谢获益提供重要见解。未来的研究还应优先检验这些饮食方案对 T1DM 和 T2DM、多囊卵巢综合征及甲状腺疾病患者的健康获益。此外，还需要在人体中直接比较隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食效果的试验，以帮助确定是否存在某一种方案优于其他方案。同样有必要开展研究，以区分减重与禁食各自对代谢参数的影响。此类研究产生的发现有助于阐明禁食所观察到的代谢改善背后的机制。

> As of 2010, only four single-​arm trials had been performed to evaluate the effects of intermittent fasting on health-​related outcomes in human participants. Over the past 10 years, interest in these diets has grown tremendously, and more data on the effects of intermittent fasting diets are beginning to emerge every month. Despite the influx of data in the past few years, much more needs to be done to increase our understanding of the field (Box 3). Specifically, long-​term (>1 year) clinical trials are critically needed. To date, the vast majority of studies in this field have been very short (between 4 and 12 weeks), with only three studies testing longterm effects (52 weeks). Randomized controlled trials and qualitative studies running for multiple years will offer important insights into the safety, feasibility and long-​term weight and cardiometabolic benefits of these protocols. Future studies should also prioritize testing the health benefits of these diets in patients with T1DM and T2DM, polycystic ovary syndrome and thyroid disorders. Trials in humans that directly compare the effects of ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE are also needed to help to ascertain whether one regimen is superior to the others. Moreover, studies that aim to tease apart the effects of weight loss versus fasting on metabolic parameters are also warranted. Findings generated from such studies could help elucidate the mechanisms that underlie the metabolic improvements observed with fasting.


### Limitations to the current evidence（p10）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/limitations-to-the-current-evidence#01 -->
**1.** 尽管这些研究为间歇性禁食改善代谢健康带来了希望，但迄今为止可获得的证据质量仍然有限。例如，这些试验大多样本量较小，仅纳入10至150名参与者。这一局限使人们质疑这些研究是否具备足够的统计效能，以检出主要和次要结局指标上具有统计学显著性的差异。此外，大多数研究是在单一地理区域开展的，即美国。还需要在世界其他地区开展更多研究，以便针对其他人群和饮食文化制定禁食建议。

> While these studies offer promise for the use of intermittent fasting in improving metabolic health, the quality of available evidence to date is limited. For instance, the majority of these trials had small sample sizes, including 10–150 participants. This limitation puts into question whether these studies were adequately powered to detect statistically significant differences in primary and secondary outcome measures. Moreover, the majority of studies were conducted in one geographical region, namely the USA. Additional research in other areas of the world is needed to tailor recommendations for fasting to other populations and food cultures.

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/limitations-to-the-current-evidence#02 -->
**2.** 我们还使用 Cochrane 协作网工具进行了偏倚风险评估。随机序列生成、分配隐藏、盲法和不完整结局数据被评定为高偏倚风险、低偏倚风险或偏倚风险不明确（补充图 1）。我们发现，在我们评估的 22 项试验中，有 15 项存在高偏倚风险，主要原因是参与者结局数据缺失。此外，没有任何一项研究对研究人员或参与者实施盲法，因为由于饮食干预的性质，这不可能做到。这些发现凸显出，需要更多把握度充足、高质量的随机对照试验，并清晰、准确地报告结局数据。

> We also performed a risk of bias assessment using the Cochrane Collaboration tool. Random sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding and incomplete outcome data were graded as having a high, low or unclear risk of bias (Supplementary Fig. 1). We found that 15 of the 22 trials we assessed were at high risk of bias, mostly as a result of missing participant outcome data. Moreover, none of the studies blinded study personnel or participants as this was not possible owing to the nature of the dietary interventions. These findings highlight the need for more well-​powered, high-​quality, randomized controlled trials that clearly and accurately report outcome data.


### Conclusions（p11）

<!-- s41574-022-00638-x#abstract/conclusions#01 -->
**1.** 间歇性禁食的健康益处正随着新证据的不断涌现而逐渐明晰。迄今为止的研究结果表明，隔日禁食、5:2 饮食和限时进食可在 8–12 周内产生轻度至中度的体重减轻（较基线减轻 3–8%）。这一减重幅度与传统饮食方法（每日热量限制）所达到的效果相当。这些间歇性禁食方案能否有助于长期体重管理，目前仍知之甚少，因为迄今为止大多数研究的持续时间都较短。间歇性禁食对心脏代谢风险指标的影响仍不确定。虽然一些研究已证明血压、LDL 胆固醇、甘油三酯、胰岛素抵抗和 HbA1c 有所改善，但另一些研究则显示对这些指标没有益处。在安全性方面，初步数据表明禁食产生的胃肠道、神经、激素或代谢不良反应很少。然而，由于在禁食的人体试验中并未定期评估不良结局，目前难以就这些饮食的安全性得出明确结论。综合来看，这些初步发现提示禁食可能对减重有效，但这些饮食在代谢紊乱预防和管理中的作用仍需进一步阐明。尽管过去 10 年该领域已取得一些进展，但仍需要来自把握度充足的长期随机对照试验的数据，以进一步阐明这些流行饮食在不同人群中的安全性和有效性。

> The health benefits of intermittent fasting are slowly being clarified as new evidence continues to emerge. Findings to date suggest that ADF, the 5:2 diet and TRE produce mild to moderate weight loss (3–8% loss from baseline) over 8–12 weeks. This degree of weight loss is on a par with that achieved with traditional dieting approaches (daily calorie restriction). The ability of these intermittent fasting protocols to help to manage weight long-​term is still poorly understood, as the majority of studies to date have run for short durations. The impact of intermittent fasting on cardiometabolic risk parameters is still uncertain. While some studies have demonstrated improvements in blood pressure, LDL cholesterol, triglycerides, insulin resistance and HbA1c, others have shown no benefit on these parameters. Regarding safety, preliminary data indicate that fasting produces few gastrointestinal, neurol ogical, hormonal or metabolic adverse effects. However, as adverse outcomes are not regularly assessed in human trials of fasting, definitive conclusions regarding the safety of these diets are difficult to draw at present. Taken together, these initial findings suggest that fasting might be effective for weight loss, but the effects of these diets in the prevention and management of metabolic disorders needs further clarification. Although some progress has been made in this field over the past 10 years, data from well-​powered, long-​term randomized controlled trials is needed to further elucidate the safety and efficacy of these popular diets in various population groups.
